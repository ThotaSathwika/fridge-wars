# Demo video script (about 2 minutes, under the 3-minute limit)

**Setup:** put the Telegram desktop app (the Fridge Wars group) on the left and the scoreboard (http://localhost:8766/site/) on the right. Keep Switch Console open. Start screen recording with your microphone on.

| Time | On screen | What you say / do |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Scoreboard title card | "This is Fridge Wars. I tell it what's in my fridge, and three AI chefs battle to cook the best dish. It runs on Switch, with four Claude Code agents in a Telegram group." |
| 0:15–0:25 | Telegram group | "Meet the cast: the Critic, Chef Nonna, Chef Blaze and Chef Zen." (scroll past their names in the chat) |
| 0:25–0:40 | Telegram, Wispr active | Type `@fridge-critic` then **dictate with Wispr**: "New battle! I've got pasta, garlic, a lemon, spinach and some parmesan." Send. |
| 0:40–1:15 | Telegram | The Critic announces the battle and challenges the chefs (Switch targeted message). The three pitches arrive. Read one trash-talk line out loud. "Notice they're talking to each other. That's Switch routing messages between agents." |
| 1:15–1:35 | Telegram | The Critic posts scores and the winner, then the recipe card. |
| 1:35–1:55 | Scoreboard | Switch to the website: the new battle appears live, with the confetti, the crown, the score bars and the leaderboard. Click **Print / Save as PDF** on the recipe card. |
| 1:55–2:10 | Telegram | Dictate: "@fridge-critic rematch, but dessert only, under ten minutes." Show the battle starting. "Every round is different, and I never touch a keyboard." |
| 2:10–2:20 | Scoreboard | "Fridge Wars: Switch, Claude Code, Telegram and Wispr. The code and setup are in the repo." |

**Tips**
- Do one practice battle first, so the agents' sessions are warm and replies come quickly.
- If a reply is slow, cut the waiting in editing.
- Upload to YouTube as **Unlisted** (or to Loom), then paste the link into the form.
